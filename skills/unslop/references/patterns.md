# Unslop Patterns

Use this catalog conservatively. A match is a review signal, not automatic
permission to edit.

## Strong Signals

- **AI preamble or meta-talk:** setup about how the answer will proceed instead
  of the answer itself.
- **Sycophancy:** unearned praise, agreement theater, or validation that carries
  no information.
- **Puffery:** inflated importance, novelty, certainty, or impact unsupported by
  evidence.
- **Vague attribution:** `experts say`, `many believe`, or `research shows`
  without a source the text can actually name.
- **Forced threes or groups of three:** ideas stretched or duplicated to fit a
  rhetorical pattern rather than the material.
- **Synonym cycling:** needless renaming of one concept to avoid repetition.
- **False ranges:** `from X to Y` where X and Y are not meaningful endpoints of
  one scale or scope.
- **Generic conclusion:** a closing paragraph that repeats the introduction or
  claims broad significance without adding a decision or next action.

## Contextual Signals

- Filler transitions, throat-clearing, and summaries that delay the first useful
  sentence.
- Abstract nouns, nominalizations, and jargon where a precise concrete verb is
  available.
- Formulaic paragraph rhythm, monotonous sentence openings, or repeated section
  templates that make the text feel generated.
- Stock AI vocabulary or metaphors that add mood but no meaning.

Do not edit a contextual signal when it is established domain language,
recipient-appropriate voice, intentional rhythm, or the clearest exact term.
